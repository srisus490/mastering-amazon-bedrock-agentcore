1. Press Ctrl + Shift + R (hard refresh)

WHERE TO FIND:

📅 DATE PRESETS:
- Location: Filter section, after date inputs
- Buttons: "Last Week", "Last 2 Weeks", "Last Month", "Last 3 Months"

🌙 THEME TOGGLE:

- Icon: 🌙 (light mode) or ☀️ (dark mode)
- Usage: Click to switch themes → preference saved automatically
